A charge over land is only as good as the land. Titles, valuations and site reports are prepared by different people at different times, and it is not unusual for them to describe a parcel slightly differently, or for the parcel on the ground to have changed since.

A verification survey checks the security itself. We start from the documents the lender holds, visit the parcel, locate the beacons and measure them, and look at access and at anything built near the boundary. The report compares what we found with the documents, line by line, with coordinates and photographs.

For a lender with many securities, the same measurements go into a GIS layer, so the portfolio can be seen on one map and checked against flood zones, reserves or planned roads.
