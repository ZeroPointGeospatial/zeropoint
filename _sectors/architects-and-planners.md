A design is only as good as the drawing it starts from. When the base survey is incomplete, in the wrong coordinate system or drawn in a way that does not open cleanly, the design team spends its first week fixing it, or worse, does not notice.

We survey with the design in mind. Before fieldwork we agree what the drawing needs to show, how dense the levels need to be and which format and layers you work in. On site we measure the ground, the structures and the services at the boundary. In the office the data is drawn into named layers, checked against the control and plotted at your scale.

For planners the same discipline applies to GIS: layers with clear attributes, consistent coordinates and a note that says where every dataset came from. That is what lets a plan be checked, updated and reused.
