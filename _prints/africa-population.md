Every column is a hexagon about 17 kilometres across, and its height is the number of people living in it. Cairo and the Nile delta rise as a wall of columns; Nigeria, the Ethiopian highlands and the shores of Lake Victoria form the great dense plains; the Sahara and the Kalahari stay almost flat. Cairo, Lagos, Kinshasa, Luanda, Nairobi and Johannesburg are named.

It is drawn the same way as the Kenya population plate, with colour starting at the same density, so the two hang together.
