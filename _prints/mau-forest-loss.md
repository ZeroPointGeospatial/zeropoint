A ray-traced model of the Mau Forest Complex and the land around it, from Kericho to the edge of the Rift, with the forest painted on. Forest with no loss mapped since 2000 is green, land that was not forest in 2000 is left the colour of the paper, and every 30 metre patch where the satellites mapped the canopy as removed is coloured by its year, from pale yellow in the early 2000s to deep red in the last few years.

In 2000, 5 410 km² of the sheet, about 43 %, had a tree canopy of 30 % or more; the data records 1 080 km² of it, a fifth, as lost by 2023. "Loss" is the dataset's term for a 30 metre cell whose canopy was removed: it includes the harvest of plantations and fires as well as clearing for farms, and it does not count regrowth, so land cut and replanted in rotation shows as a patchwork of years. The bars in the key show the area mapped each year; the data's makers warn that years before 2011 were mapped with an earlier method, so single years should not be compared closely.
