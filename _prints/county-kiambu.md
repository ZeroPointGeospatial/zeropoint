The ridges and valleys running down from the Kikuyu Escarpment to the plains around Thika and Ruiru.

The county is cut to its boundary and modelled like the national relief plate, with its own colour range so the shape of the land shows. It carries the county's official number, 022. Any of the 47 counties can be made the same way.
