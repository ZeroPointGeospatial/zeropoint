Lake Magadi as Copernicus Sentinel-2 saw it on 14 September 2025: pink brine in the southern lagoons, the white crust of the pan, green water in the north-west lagoon, and a separate green lake a few kilometres to the north. Red-brown fault blocks of the Rift floor run on either side, with the green floodplain of the Southern Ewaso Ng'iro in the west and Mount Olorgesailie in the north-east corner.

The colours are the satellite's own red, green and blue measurements of the ground at 10 metres, with a gentle tone curve that keeps the whites of the pan from turning flat; they are laid over the 30 metre Copernicus elevation model and ray-traced in plan view, three times exaggerated. The scene was chosen from the clear passes of two years for the colour of the lake, and there is no cloud on it.
