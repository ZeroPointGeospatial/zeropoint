We did not download this river network. We computed it. Fifty-one million elevation cells were flooded and routed downhill to find where water collects, with the Rift lakes, the Chalbi and the calderas kept as closed basins the way they are on the ground.

Line weight follows the water, not the size of the catchment: thirty years of rainfall are turned into runoff and accumulated downstream, so the Tana and the rivers of the west run bright while the dry channels of the north stay as fine threads. The Ewaso Ng'iro ends at the Lorian Swamp, as it does in most years. Colour shows which of Kenya's five drainage basins the water belongs to.
