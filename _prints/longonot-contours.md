Contours at 20 metres with labelled index contours every 100, inside a neat line that carries UTM grid ticks the way a topographic sheet does. No shading and no colour: only the form of the crater, the rim and the gullies down the cone.

The contours are interpolated from a 30 metre surface model. It is a drawing of the mountain, not a ground survey.
