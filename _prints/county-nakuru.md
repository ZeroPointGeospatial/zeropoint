Lakes Nakuru, Elmenteita and Naivasha on the Rift floor, with Menengai, Longonot and the Mau Escarpment.

The county is cut to its boundary and modelled like the national relief plate, with its own colour range so the shape of the land shows. It carries the county's official number, 032. Any of the 47 counties can be made the same way.
