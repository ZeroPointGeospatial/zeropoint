Every column is a 400 metre hexagon, and its height is the number of people living in it. The sheet is a block centred on Lake Victoria, with the shores of Uganda, Kenya and Tanzania; it holds 43.4 million people, and just over half of them live within 25 km of the lake. Mwanza, Kisumu and Kampala rise as three towers of similar height, the tallest a single hexagon in Mwanza with 19 428 people. Kampala, Entebbe, Jinja, Busia, Kisumu, Musoma, Mwanza and Bukoba are named.

Columns standing out in the open lake are on islands, many too small to show in the lake outline; those at the water's edge stand a little off the shore because the outline is coarse. The model is drawn the same way as the Kenya and Africa population plates, with colour starting at the same density, so the three hang together.
