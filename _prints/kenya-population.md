Every column is a 400 metre hexagon, and its height is the number of people living in it. Nairobi, Mombasa and Kisumu rise as towers, and are named at the foot of their columns. The farmland of western Kenya and the central highlands forms a dense carpet, and the north stays almost flat, broken by the needles of its towns and settlements.

The model is lit like a physical object and ray-traced, so every column casts a real shadow across the country.
