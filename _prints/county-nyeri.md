The county between two mountains: the Aberdare Range to the west and the slopes of Mount Kenya, up to Batian, to the east.

The county is cut to its boundary and modelled like the national relief plate, with its own colour range so the shape of the land shows. It carries the county's official number, 019. Any of the 47 counties can be made the same way.
