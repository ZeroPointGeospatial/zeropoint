Every road and track in OpenStreetMap, about 392 000 km of them. Local roads are drawn as density, so towns glow and the forests of Mount Kenya, the Aberdares and the Mau show up as dark gaps in the network. Trunk, primary, secondary and tertiary roads are drawn over the top as lines, and two dozen towns are named, from Lodwar and Mandera to Namanga and Lamu.

It uses the deep green and gold of the Nairobi city prints, and the same scale as the rivers and relief plates.
