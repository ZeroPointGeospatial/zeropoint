The summit of Mount Kenya at walking-map scale: Batian and Point Lenana at the centre, with the Teleki, Mackinder, Gorges and Hobley valleys running out from them. Contours at 20 metres, index contours every 100, inside a neat line with UTM grid ticks.

A companion to the Longonot study, and to the Mount Kenya relief plate. The contours are interpolated from a 30 metre surface model; it is a drawing, not a ground survey.
