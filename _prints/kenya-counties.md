A reference sheet for an office or a classroom. Each county is tinted so that neighbours differ, marked with its official county number and named where the name fits. All 47 are listed by number in the corner below the border, so the small counties of the west and centre are easy to find.

The boundaries come from OpenStreetMap and are right for a wall map. They are not a statement about where any boundary legally runs.
