The floor of the central Rift between the Mau escarpment and the Aberdares. The valley floor is kept in sand tones and the shoulders in green, so the trough reads at a glance. Lakes Nakuru, Elmenteita and Naivasha lie along it, with the calderas of Menengai, Longonot and Suswa, and Eburru between them.

Lesatima and Kinangop, the high points of the Aberdares, are on the eastern edge.
