The region as a single relief model: the eastern Rift running south through Kenya and Tanzania, the western Rift with Lakes Albert, Tanganyika and Malawi, and Lake Victoria in the basin between them. Kilimanjaro, Mount Kenya, the Rwenzori, Meru, Karisimbi and Elgon are marked with their heights.

No borders are drawn. It is a map of the land, lit from the north-west and ray-traced like the other relief plates.
