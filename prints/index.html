02How they are made

Mapped by engineers, not a poster shop

Each plate starts as data and goes through the same handling as our survey work: a stated source, a stated projection and a method we can explain.

Source

Open, citable data

Copernicus elevation models at 30 and 90 metres, OpenStreetMap roads and boundaries, Kontur population, CHIRPS rainfall and Hansen tree cover. The source and licence are printed on each atlas, landform and county plate.

Processing

Our own analysis

The river network is computed from the terrain rather than downloaded, and each river is weighted by the flow that thirty years of rainfall would give it. Relief and population are built as 3D models and ray-traced so shadows fall the way they would on a physical model.

Sheet

Scale and projection stated

Country plates are drawn in UTM zone 37S at 1:2 400 000 on A2, which is 1:1 200 000 on A0. Each sheet carries its scale, centre coordinates and plate number.
