02How they are made

One pipeline, every county

Each plate starts from the Copernicus elevation model, at 30 metres for the smaller counties and 90 metres for the large ones in the north and east. The terrain is cut to the county boundary, coloured by height across the county's own range, so a flat county still shows its shape, and ray-traced with a low sun from the north-west.

The scale is chosen to fill the sheet and printed on it, with the projection and the sheet centre. County boundaries come from OpenStreetMap and are right for a wall map; they are not a statement about where any boundary legally runs.
